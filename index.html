Land Claiming Guide (GriefPrevention)

We use GriefPrevention to protect your builds from griefers. Placing your very first chest will automatically create a small claim around it!

  1. Hold a Golden Shovel in your hand.
  2. Right-click one corner of the land you want to protect.
  3. Right-click the opposite corner to finish the claim.

Tip: Right-click with a Stick to see claim boundaries, and use /trust <name> to let your friends build with you.

Rules

  1. Don't be an asshole.
  2. Mods/resource packs that provide an unfair advantage are not allowed (e.g. X-Ray, Flight).
  3. Mods like schematica or litematica are OK, provided that you don't use the Easy Place or similar feature.
  4. Players are kindly requested not to build massive farms with many entities as this could degrade the experience.
